# Synthetic Change Complexity Risk Guidance

Synthetic policy used only for the demo, informed by FATF's risk-based
approach to assessing uncertainty and control ability (see
`docs/requirements/supervisory-frameworks.md`). Source identifier:
`change-complexity-v1`.

## Ambiguous or contradictory submissions

Where a change request contains contradictory or incomplete information,
such as conflicting statements about geography, audience, or vendor status,
treat the ambiguity itself as a risk-increasing factor until an analyst
resolves it, rather than assuming the more favorable interpretation.

## Stated risk factors

Explicit risk factors named in the source submission, such as rapid funds
movement, sanctions exposure, or cross-border payment flows, should
increase the assessed risk level even when no other category is triggered.

## Confidence and control ability

Lower confidence in the underlying facts reduces the organization's ability
to identify and control the resulting risk. A low-confidence extraction
should trigger closer analyst review, not be averaged away by other, more
confident signals.
