# Synthetic Product and Channel Risk Guidance

Synthetic policy used only for the demo, informed by BSA/AML product- and
channel-risk principles (see `docs/requirements/supervisory-frameworks.md`).
Source identifier: `product-risk-v1`.

## New products and features

New products, and material changes to existing products, require a
documented risk analysis covering how the product could be misused for
money laundering, terrorist financing, or fraud, before approval.

## Delivery channel risk

Digital, remote, and rapid-funds-movement channels carry different risk
characteristics than in-person, relationship-based channels. A change that
introduces a new delivery channel, or removes an existing control point in
the customer journey, requires explicit review of the resulting exposure.

## Multi-product exposure

Changes that touch more than one product concurrently should be reviewed
for compounding exposure across products, not assessed as isolated,
independent changes.
