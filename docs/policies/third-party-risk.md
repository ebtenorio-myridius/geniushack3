# Synthetic Third-Party and Vendor Risk Guidance

Synthetic policy used only for the demo, informed by outsourcing and
third-party risk-management principles (see
`docs/requirements/supervisory-frameworks.md`). Source identifier:
`third-party-risk-v1`.

## Vendor due diligence

Vendor involvement in a change requires documented due diligence on the
vendor's own financial-crime controls, ownership, and sanctions exposure
before onboarding or expanding the relationship.

## Ongoing oversight

Third-party arrangements require ongoing monitoring and a named internal
owner accountable for the vendor relationship. A change cannot rely solely
on point-in-time diligence performed at onboarding.

## Concentration and dependency

Where a vendor performs a function that is difficult to replace or
supervise, such as core processing or document handling for regulated
products, treat that concentration itself as a risk factor distinct from
the vendor's individual control quality.
