# Synthetic Customer and Geography Risk Guidance

Synthetic policy used only for the demo, informed by BSA/AML customer- and
geography-risk principles (see `docs/requirements/supervisory-frameworks.md`).
Source identifier: `customer-risk-v1`.

## Customer segment exposure

Assess whether the change introduces or expands exposure to customer
segments associated with higher financial-crime risk, such as cash-intensive
businesses, non-resident customers, or politically exposed persons. A new or
materially changed customer segment requires a documented risk rationale
before launch.

## Geographic footprint

Assess cross-border exposure and country risk, including jurisdictions
subject to sanctions programs or identified as higher-risk by recognized
international bodies. Expansion into a new jurisdiction, or one with
elevated country risk, requires enhanced review.

## Aggregation of exposure

Where a change touches multiple customer segments or geographies at once,
assess the combined exposure rather than each factor in isolation.
Combined exposure can exceed what any single factor implies on its own.
